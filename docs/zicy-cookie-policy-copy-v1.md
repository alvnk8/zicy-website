## 1. The short version

- Cookies that keep you signed in and keep the service secure are always on. The site cannot work without them.
- Everything else is off until you say yes.
- You can change your mind at any time using **Cookie settings** in the footer.
- We do not use cookies to sell your data or to build advertising profiles about you.

---

## 2. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device so it can recognise your browser on a later visit.

We use the word cookie loosely in this policy to also cover similar technologies, which do a comparable job:

- **Local storage and session storage**, which hold data in your browser rather than in a cookie file
- **Pixels and tags**, small pieces of code that record that a page or an email was opened
- **Software development kits**, code inside our Chrome extension and WordPress plugin

## 3. The categories we use

### Strictly necessary

These are required for the service to function and are set without consent, because you cannot use Zicy without them.

They do things like keep you signed in across pages, remember which brand profile is active, protect forms against cross-site request forgery, balance server load, and enforce your plan's limits.

### Functional

These remember preferences so the product behaves the way you left it. Examples are the light and dark theme toggle, your language, your dismissal of a banner, and your saved filters.

Turning these off does not break Zicy, but it will keep forgetting your choices.

### Analytics

These tell us which pages and features are used, where people get stuck, and which errors are firing. We use this to fix and improve the product.

### Marketing

These measure whether a campaign worked and let us reach people who visited the site. [FACT-C03] Delete this whole category from the page if Zicy sets no marketing cookies. Do not publish an empty category.

## 6. How to change your choices

- **On our site:** open **Cookie settings** in the footer. Your choice is stored and applied on every later visit. [FACT-C07] Confirm the consent tool and that the link works.
- **In your browser:** every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop Zicy from working.
- **Global Privacy Control:** [FACT-C08] State whether Zicy honours the GPC signal. If it does not, delete this bullet rather than claiming it.

Clearing your cookies also clears your stored consent choice, so you will be asked again on your next visit.

## 7. Consent, and where it applies

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before setting any cookie that is not strictly necessary, and we do not treat continued browsing as consent.

If you are in Malaysia, Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended, or in California, we still give you the same controls. We think one standard is simpler than three.

You can withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it.

## 8. The Chrome extension and the WordPress plugin

- **Chrome extension:** it analyses a page only when you ask it to. It does not track your browsing history and it does not sell or share data with third parties. [FACT-C09] Confirm what it stores locally in the browser.
- **WordPress plugin:** it syncs AI crawler access logs from your own server. It does not set cookies on your visitors. [FACT-C10] Confirm this.
